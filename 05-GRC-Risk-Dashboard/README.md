# GRC Risk Dashboard

## Overview

A simulated GRC risk dashboard for a fictional financial services organization.

The project demonstrates how a GRC / IT Risk professional can consolidate risk information, analyze risk exposure, track remediation activities, and present risk insights to management.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a simulated portfolio project. All organizations, risks, data, findings, ratings, and recommendations are fictional and created for demonstration purposes.

## Project Objectives

- Consolidate cybersecurity and operational risks
- Assess likelihood and business impact
- Prioritize risks using a defined scoring methodology
- Track risk owners and remediation status
- Identify high-priority risk areas
- Create an executive-level risk dashboard
- Demonstrate Excel-based GRC reporting skills

## Key Deliverables

- Enterprise risk register
- Risk scoring methodology
- Risk heat map
- Risk status analysis
- Remediation tracking
- Executive risk dashboard
- Executive summary report

## Skills Demonstrated

- Risk identification
- Risk assessment
- Risk scoring
- Risk prioritization
- Risk treatment
- Remediation tracking
- Excel data analysis
- Executive reporting
- GRC risk management
