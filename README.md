# GRC & IT Risk Cybersecurity Portfolio

A hands-on portfolio demonstrating practical Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), IT Risk, cybersecurity risk management, Identity and Access Management (IAM), third-party risk, and security reporting skills.

## About This Portfolio

This portfolio contains simulated cybersecurity and IT Risk projects designed to demonstrate how GRC professionals assess risk, evaluate controls, identify gaps, document findings, and develop remediation recommendations.

The projects use fictional organizations, systems, users, evidence, and assessment results for demonstration purposes.

## Portfolio Methodology

Projects generally follow a structured GRC workflow:

**Assess → Identify Gaps → Analyze Risk → Prioritize → Recommend → Remediate → Report**

The goal is to demonstrate the ability to translate cybersecurity requirements, control assessments, and risk findings into practical business decisions and remediation actions.
