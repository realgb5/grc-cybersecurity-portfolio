# NIST CSF 2.0 Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Overview

A simulated cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional financial services organization aligned with the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**.

The project demonstrates how a GRC / IT Risk professional can assess cybersecurity controls, identify gaps between current and target states, prioritize risks, and develop a practical remediation roadmap.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a simulated portfolio project. The organization, assessment data, findings, evidence, risks, and recommendations are fictional and created for demonstration purposes.

---

## Project Objectives

- Assess the organization's cybersecurity posture
- Evaluate current and target cybersecurity states
- Identify cybersecurity control gaps
- Identify and prioritize material risks
- Develop risk treatment recommendations
- Create a remediation roadmap
- Present findings in an executive-friendly format

---

## Framework

**NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**

The assessment considers the six NIST CSF 2.0 Functions:

- Govern
- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

---

## Assessment Approach

The assessment followed a simplified GRC risk assessment workflow:

**NIST CSF Category → Current State → Target State → Gap → Risk → Recommendation → Remediation**

The assessment considered areas including:

- Governance and oversight
- Risk management
- Asset management
- Risk assessment
- Identity and access management
- Data security
- Protective technology
- Continuous monitoring
- Incident response
- Recovery planning
- Third-party risk

---

## Key Findings

The simulated assessment identified several areas requiring remediation, including:

| Risk Area | Priority | Example Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Identity & Access Management | Critical | Unauthorized access to employee accounts |
| Data Security | High | Potential exposure of sensitive customer information |
| Ransomware Resilience | High | Business disruption from ransomware |
| Third-Party Risk | High | Security incidents involving third-party providers |
| Detection & Monitoring | High | Delayed identification of security events |
| Privileged Access | High | Excessive user privileges |
| Asset Management | High | Gaps in technology asset visibility |
| Regulatory Reporting | Medium | Delays in regulatory/security reporting |

---

## Risk Assessment

A **5x5 likelihood and impact model** was used to prioritize risks.

Risk score:

**Likelihood × Impact = Risk Score**

Risk ratings were categorized as:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

> The 5x5 scoring model is a portfolio methodology used for this simulated assessment and is **not prescribed by NIST CSF 2.0**.
